FAQ

How do I tell whether my article was cited?

Look at the URLs and site names in the "Sources cited" table. Links that appear inside the answer text are not always citations.

Why does the answer change every time?

The AI writes the text fresh, so both wording and sources can differ even for the same question. Checking the same question on different days shows the trend.

Can the sources list be short or empty?

Yes. It varies: our US test ("best affiliate networks") listed 11 sources, an earlier test in Japan with a Japanese keyword listed 2, and for some answers there may be none.

How much does it cost and how long does it take?

About $0.004 per request, and about 7 seconds in our US test (about 15 seconds in an earlier test in Japan). There is no option to choose a count.
