Why use this tool

  • Tendencies of people who search a keyword. No site of your own needed.
  • About $0.0024. About 400 per $1, so you can check each post idea.
  • About 1 second.
  • Works for the US and other countries. "side hustle" in the US returned age and gender (measured). A test with "副業" in Japan also worked.
  • Save as CSV.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want percentages → the values are relative to the largest group (set to 100); they do not add up to 100%.
  • You want people aged 55 and over → the age groups are 18-24, 25-34, 35-44 and 45-54. Older groups are not returned (measured).
  • You want head counts → this tool cannot show them.
  • You want the profile of site visitors → this is about people who search the keyword. For site visitors, SimilarWeb and similar tools fit better.

This tool does one thing: show the age and gender tendencies of people who search a keyword.

FAQ

What do the numbers mean?

They are relative to the largest group, which is 100. For "side hustle" in the US, 25-34 was 100, 18-24 was 96, female was 100 and male was 79.

Why is there no 55+ group?

The API returns four groups: 18-24, 25-34, 35-44 and 45-54.

Why is there no language selector?

This API accepts a country only.

Does every keyword work?

Rarely searched words may return no numbers. Then the table is empty.
