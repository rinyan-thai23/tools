Why use this tool

  • See the site's search strengths. For target.com, the biggest category (ID 10021) had about 1.17 million keywords and about 4.7 million in estimated traffic (measured).
  • About $0.024 per call. About 40 per $1, so you can research several prospects.
  • A few seconds. 1.5 seconds in the sample (measured).
  • Category names are shown. They are in English.
  • Save as CSV.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want the company's industry → categories are the API's own classification, not the company's sector.
  • You want category names in another language → names come in English with the ID. If the free reference list cannot be fetched, only the ID is shown.
  • You want real visit counts → Estimated traffic (ETV) is a modeled estimate from rankings and search volume (per DataForSEO), not measured visits, and the calculation may be updated (see the official site).
  • Unnamed classes such as "Unknown" can also appear (seen in a test in Japan with suumo.jp).

This tool does one thing: show up to 100 categories a domain appears in, with keyword counts.

FAQ

Where do the category IDs and names come from?

The IDs come from the main API call. The names come from a classification list DataForSEO publishes for free (in English). Fetching that list costs nothing.

What is "estimated traffic"?

Estimated traffic (ETV) is a modeled estimate from rankings and search volume (per DataForSEO), not measured visits, and the calculation may be updated (see the official site).

Can I see more than 100?

No. target.com had 3,035 categories in total (measured).

What do country and language change?

The market for the numbers. The sample uses the United States and English, so the numbers are for US search results.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
