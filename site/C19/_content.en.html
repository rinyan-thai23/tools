Why use this tool

  • Read the answer as text. See whether you or your rivals are named.
  • About $0.002 and 4 seconds. About 450 per $1, so you can try many questions.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want fresh information or sources → this tool does not use web search. Claude answers from what it already knows and shows no sources (web search cost about $0.025 in our test).
  • You want exactly what the Claude app shows → this asks claude-haiku-4-5 through the API. The model and answer can differ from the app.
  • You want a verdict on whether you were named → the tool just lays out the answer.
  • You want ongoing tracking → one call is one answer at one moment.

FAQ

Why are there no sources?

The tool does not use web search. For sources, the Perplexity and Gemini tools fit better.

Does the cost depend on the answer length?

We measured about $0.002 (two calls: $0.0022 and $0.0024). The cost for a long answer is untested.

Why is there no country or language selector?

This call has no country or language setting.

Is the answer the same each time?

Answers can change from call to call.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
