Why use this tool

  • Works on other people's blog domains. See a rival's main earners.
  • Top 10 only, by volume. target.com had about 3.0 million organic top-10 keywords; we took the 100 with the highest volume (measured).
  • Position and URL included. See which page ranks where.
  • About $0.024 per call. About 40 per $1.
  • Save as CSV.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want all positions → it is limited to the top 10. Positions 11 and below are not shown.
  • You want more than 100 → it stops at 100.
  • You want promising words → sorted by volume, the top rows are brand, store and product names (for target.com, "target", "targets", "needohs" and "store near me" led, and "target" ranked 1st).
  • You want real visits → volume is the keyword's monthly searches, not the visitors the site received.

This tool does one thing: show the 100 highest-volume keywords a domain ranks in the top 10 for.

FAQ

Why limit it to the top 10?

To serve as a "keywords it ranks high for" tool, we limit it to the top 10 and sort by volume. That limit is this tool's own rule.

Does the position include ads?

No. Only organic results (not ads) are included, with the position within organic results (rank_group).

Why only 100?

The tool fixes the count at 100 per call.

What do country and language change?

The market for the numbers. The sample uses the United States and English, so positions are in US results.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
