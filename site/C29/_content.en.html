When it is not the right fit

  • You want related topics or related queries → this tool returns the popularity trend only (confirmed in our test).
  • You want search counts → popularity is relative, with the peak in the period set to 100. It is not a count.
  • You want more than a year → it covers about the last year (53 weeks).
  • With several keywords, the numbers appear in the order you entered them, like "45 / 36".
  • You want a quick result → we measured about 8 to 20 seconds.

This tool does one thing: fetch the weekly Google Trends popularity of your keywords.

FAQ

What is the "100" in popularity?

The most popular week in the period is set to 100. It is not a search count.

How do I read several keywords?

They appear in the order you entered, separated by slashes. "45 / 36" means 45 for the first and 36 for the second.

How many keywords can I enter?

Up to 5. Anything beyond the fifth is not sent.

How many weeks are shown?

53 weeks in our sample.
