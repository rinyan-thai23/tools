Why use this tool

  • Counts per emotion. For "ユニクロ": about 1.53 million for love, 82 thousand for happiness and 57 thousand for fun (measured).
  • Counts for the three groups too. Positive, negative and neutral mention counts line up.
  • About $0.024 per call. Roughly 40 per $1.
  • About 23 seconds. That was the first measurement; repeating the same word sometimes returned in a few seconds.
  • Save as CSV. Groundwork for a report.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want Japanese pages only → this analysis is not filtered by language.
  • You want numbers above 4 million → counts stop at 4,000,000. The positive count for "ユニクロ" showed 4,000,000 (measured).
  • You want percentages → this tool shows counts only. When a count stops at its ceiling, a percentage would be wrong.
  • You want the individual pages → "See web mentions" fits better.

This tool does one thing: show mention counts per positive/negative/neutral group and per emotion.

FAQ

What are the six emotions?

Happiness, love, fun, sadness, anger and share (the urge to share). This is DataForSEO's classification.

Why counts and not percentages?

Some counts stop at the 4,000,000 ceiling, so a percentage would be inaccurate.

How does it differ from "See a summary of mentions"?

That one shows total count, page types, languages and top domains. This one shows counts per positive/negative/neutral group and per emotion.

Did a person judge the sentiment?

No. DataForSEO analysed the pages' text.
