FAQ

Does the table match the citations inside the answer?

The table is the list of search results the API returned. We do not map them to the numbered citations in the answer.

Why is there no web-search setting?

Perplexity always uses search.

Why is there no country or language selector?

This call has no country or language setting.

Does the cost depend on the answer length?

Yes. We measured about $0.006.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
