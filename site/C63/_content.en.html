Why use this tool

  • The whole page becomes one image. The blog article we tried came out as an image 1,920 px wide and 43,912 px tall (measured).
  • About $0.0048 per call. Roughly 200 per $1, so you can compare several rivals regularly.
  • About 18 to 23 seconds. That is what we measured.
  • Keep the image URL. The CSV contains it.
  • Works on Japanese pages. The text appeared as it should.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want to keep the image for a long time → the image URL may expire (see the official site). Save important images yourself.
  • You want a mobile-width image → this tool returns a desktop-width image.
  • You want automatic daily records → this tool takes one shot when you press the button.
  • The image is large (about 6 MB for the page we tried), so mind your data use. Ads and parts that load later may not appear.

This tool does one thing: take one full-page screenshot of one page.

FAQ

Where is the image stored?

On DataForSEO's side. Right-click the image on screen and save it yourself.

What if the image does not show?

Try again after a while. The image URL is also in the CSV.

Isn't the image too long?

It covers the whole page, so it is tall. The screen shows the top part; save the image to see all of it.

Does the price change with page length?

We tried one page. The effect of length is untested.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
