Why use this tool

  • The skeleton of an article at a glance. The article we tried (shopify.com/blog/how-to-start-a-blog) came out as 42 headings with their text (measured).
  • Text sits next to its heading. Read what appears under a heading in one row.
  • About $0.00015 per call. Roughly 6,600 per $1, so you can check as many articles as you like.
  • A few seconds. The sample came back in under a second.
  • Save as CSV. Use it as groundwork when you plan your own headings.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want to copy the article as is → reusing someone else's writing raises copyright issues. Use this only to learn structure.
  • You need images or table contents → the table shows headings and text only.
  • You want word count or load time → "See a page's basic facts" fits better.
  • The split between main and other content is DataForSEO's own call, so the order can differ from top-to-bottom on the page.

This tool does one thing: pull an article's headings (H1 to H4) and the text under them.

FAQ

Why is the order not top to bottom?

DataForSEO returns the page split into main content and other content. This tool lists the main part first.

What happens with long text?

The sentences under a heading are joined into one cell. For long ones, save as CSV and read it in a spreadsheet.

May I use other people's articles?

Looking at them for research rarely causes trouble, but copying text and publishing it raises copyright issues. Ask a professional if you are unsure.

Does it work on any page?

We tried one blog article. Other pages may come back differently.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
