Why use this tool

  • Check a whole list at once. Line up your prospects and compare their size.
  • About $0.012 for 3 domains. Cheaper than checking each one separately.
  • A clean two-column table. Domain and estimated traffic.
  • Under a few seconds.
  • Save as CSV. Sort in a spreadsheet to prioritize your outreach.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want real visit counts → estimated traffic is a modeled figure from rankings and search volume (per DataForSEO), not real visitors.
  • You want ads (paid) numbers → only organic search is shown.
  • You also want keyword counts or positions → this table shows estimated traffic only. For one site at a time, "See a site's estimated traffic" fits better.
  • The price for up to 100 domains is a guide. We measured up to 3.

This tool does one thing: show the estimated search traffic of several domains at once.

FAQ

How many domains can I enter?

DataForSEO's spec says up to 1000 per call (100 in our plan). We confirmed up to 3 here.

How do I write the domains?

One per line, like "example.com".

Does the price change with the number of domains?

Yes. 3 domains cost about $0.012 (measured).

What do country and language change?

The market for the numbers. Japan and Japanese give numbers for Japanese results.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
