Why use this tool

  • 100 rows in one call. Searching "ラーメン" in Tokyo returned 100 rows in about 3 to 6 seconds (measured).
  • Phone and website included. Of those 100 rows, 87 had a phone number, 90 had a website and 95 had a rating (measured).
  • About $0.048 per call. Roughly 20 per $1, so you can try many areas.
  • Just pick the area from a list. No need to look up latitude and longitude.
  • Save as CSV. A draft for your sales list.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want Japanese spellings → many rows have English names, addresses and categories (e.g. "Koto City", "Ramen restaurant").
  • You want to narrow exactly by business type → the type word is a text search. Of the 100 rows for "ラーメン", 70 had the category ramen restaurant (measured). Parking lots and the like are mixed in.
  • You want a town not on the list → the area is chosen from 12 major cities.
  • You want more than 100 → this tool stops at 100. The matches for "ラーメン" around Tokyo Station were 624 (measured).

This tool does one thing: list up to 100 businesses in a chosen area that match a business-type word.

FAQ

How wide is the area?

A 5 km radius around the centre of the chosen city. For Tokyo, the centre is Tokyo Station.

Why are some rows in English?

In our test all 100 categories were in English, 29 names and 59 addresses were in Japanese. The data is probably stored that way, but we have not confirmed why.

How do I write the business-type word?

An ordinary word such as "ラーメン" or "美容室". Businesses whose description matches the word appear.

What do blank phone or website cells mean?

Nothing was registered. Of the 100 rows, 13 had no phone and 10 had no website (measured).
