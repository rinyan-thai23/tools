Why use this tool

  • Find rivals by shared keywords. For suumo.jp, yahoo.co.jp and homes.co.jp each shared about 250 thousand (measured).
  • Whole-site estimated traffic included. See how big they are.
  • About $0.024 per call. About 40 per $1.

  • Save as CSV.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want only business rivals → the list is sites that appear for the same words. Big portals and YouTube are included. Semrush also notes SEO competitors can differ from business rivals.
  • The first row is your own domain → the top row is the domain you entered. We show it as returned.
  • You want more than 100 → it stops at 100 (suumo.jp had about 458 thousand in total).
  • The estimated traffic in the table is a whole-site figure. It is a modeled number, not real visits.

This tool does one thing: show 100 sites that share keywords with a domain, with shared-keyword counts.

FAQ

Why does my own domain come first?

The API includes the domain you entered. We show it as returned.

What is the "shared keywords" count?

The number of keywords for which both your domain and that site appear in results.

Is estimated traffic for the shared keywords only?

No. It is the whole site's estimated traffic.

What do country and language change?

The market for the numbers. Japan and Japanese give numbers for Japanese results.

Can I sign up with a free email address?
