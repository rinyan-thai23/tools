When it is not the right fit

  • You want the rankings the site really holds → these are keywords the API returns as related to the site. Whether they are rankings, this tool cannot tell.
  • You want more than 100 rows → it stops at 100. suumo.jp had about 12 million in total.
  • You want a table with every difficulty filled in → DataForSEO says keyword difficulty needs SERP data, so it is only available for keywords in its SERP database. In our tests, blanks leaned toward rarely searched words (for C34 the median volume was 20 on blank rows and 390 on rows with a value).
  • Many rows are words unique to the site, such as company and property names (suumo.jp example).

This tool does one thing: show 100 keywords related to a domain, with volume and difficulty.

FAQ

Why are many difficulty values blank?

DataForSEO says keyword difficulty needs SERP data, so it is only available for keywords in its SERP database. In our tests, blanks leaned toward rarely searched words (for C34 the median volume was 20 on blank rows and 390 on rows with a value).

Is this a list of keywords the site ranks for?

No. These are candidates the API returns as related to the site, not the site's rankings.

Why only 100?

The tool fixes the count at 100 per call. 100 rows cost $0.024.

What do country and language change?

The market for the numbers. Japan and Japanese give Japanese volumes.
