When it is not the right fit

  • You want the rankings the site really holds → these are keywords the API returns as related to the site. Whether they are rankings, this tool cannot tell.
  • You want more than 100 rows → it stops at 100. target.com had about 1.1 million in total.
  • You want a table with every difficulty filled in → DataForSEO says keyword difficulty needs SERP data, so it is only available for keywords in its SERP database.In our target.com test, 7 of the 30 rows kept in the sample were blank. The median volume was 10 on blank rows and 90 on rows with a value, so blanks leaned toward rarely searched words.
  • Many rows are words unique to the site, such as store names and places (target.com example: "williamsburg general store" and "target store 3991").

This tool does one thing: show 100 keywords related to a domain, with volume and difficulty.

FAQ

Why are many difficulty values blank?

DataForSEO says keyword difficulty needs SERP data, so it is only available for keywords in its SERP database.In our target.com test, 7 of the 30 rows kept in the sample were blank. The median volume was 10 on blank rows and 90 on rows with a value, so blanks leaned toward rarely searched words.

Is this a list of keywords the site ranks for?

No. These are candidates the API returns as related to the site, not the site's rankings.

Why only 100?

The tool fixes the count at 100 per call. 100 rows cost $0.024.

What do country and language change?

The market for the numbers. The United States and English give US English volumes.
