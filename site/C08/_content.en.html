When it is not the right fit

  • You want to expand suggestions in bulk with letter prefixes → a dedicated tool such as Rakko Keyword fits better.
  • You want the search volume of each suggestion → this tool lists suggestions only.
  • A few free lookups a day are enough → a free tier or the Google search box itself will do.

FAQ

How many suggestions appear?

In our test there were 13. The number Google returns varies by keyword. There is no option to choose a count.

How is it different from Rakko Keyword?

Rakko Keyword is strong at expansion and bulk export. This tool shows the suggestion list for one word, on a pay-per-use basis.

When are the suggestions from?

They are what Google returned at the moment you pressed the button, and they can change with time and place.

Some suggestions look oddly written.

The suggestions are shown exactly as Google returned them. Our sample run included one with a broken word break. Treat the order as a rough guide and pick suggestions that make sense for your topic.

How can I use the suggestions when planning an article?

They hint at what readers tend to look up next. For "blog how to start", for instance, the list shows which words follow. The tool only lists suggestions; it does not judge which are good.
