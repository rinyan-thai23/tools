FAQ

How many suggestions appear?

In our test ("how to start a blog") there were 15. The number Google returns varies by keyword. There is no option to choose a count.

How is it different from typing into the Google search box?

You get the suggestion list for one word as a table you can save as CSV, with no copying by hand, and you can pick the country and language. It is the same kind of suggestions, paid per use.

When are the suggestions from?

They are what Google returned at the moment you pressed the button, and they can change with time and place.

Some suggestions look oddly written.

The suggestions are shown exactly as Google returned them. Our sample run for "how to start a blog" included entries such as "how to start a blog post" and "how to start a blog page" that read as off-topic. Treat the order as a rough guide and pick suggestions that make sense for your topic.

How can I use the suggestions when planning an article?

They hint at what readers tend to look up next. For "how to start a blog", for instance, the list shows words such as "for free", "and make money" and "on wordpress". The tool only lists suggestions; it does not judge which are good.
