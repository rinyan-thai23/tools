Why use this tool

  • The past 12 months in one call. You can look back even if you never kept records.
  • Counts as DataForSEO tallies them. For "ユニクロ" the figure moved from about 59 thousand in April 2026 to about 523 thousand in October (measured).
  • Sentiment counts per month. Positive, negative and neutral counts line up month by month.
  • About $0.0244 per call. Roughly 40 per $1, so you can try many words.
  • Save as CSV. Turn it into a chart in a spreadsheet.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want a trend for Japanese pages only → this trend is not filtered by language. We tried a filter and the numbers did not change (measured).
  • You want search volume over time → this counts mentions on pages, not searches. Google Trends fits better.
  • You want daily movement → this tool is monthly.
  • The current month is partial and comes out small, so it is left out of the table. The table shows the last 12 complete months.

This tool does one thing: show a keyword's mention count and sentiment counts for each of the last 12 months.

FAQ

Why monthly?

This tool is set to return 12 months, month by month. There is no daily or weekly switch.

What is the unit of the mention count?

The number of mentions as DataForSEO tallies them. See DataForSEO's documentation for the exact counting rules.

Can I limit it to Japanese?

We tried a filter and the numbers did not change, so this tool does not filter.

How long does it take?

We measured about 21 seconds.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
