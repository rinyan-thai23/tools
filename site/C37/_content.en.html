When it is not the right fit

  • You want only close words → topics far from yours also mix in. The first row for "ブログ 始め方" was "まとめの サイト" with volume 450,000 (measured).
  • You want a table with every difficulty filled in → DataForSEO says keyword difficulty needs SERP data, so it is only available for keywords in its SERP database. In our tests, blanks leaned toward rarely searched words (for C34 the median volume was 20 on blank rows and 390 on rows with a value).
  • You want more than 100 → it stops at 100.
  • Some rows have no volume (2 of 100, measured).

FAQ

Why do loosely related words appear?

We show what the API returns as the same genre, unfiltered.

Why are many difficulty values blank?

DataForSEO says keyword difficulty needs SERP data, so it is only available for keywords in its SERP database. In our tests, blanks leaned toward rarely searched words (for C34 the median volume was 20 on blank rows and 390 on rows with a value).

What does a blank volume mean?

The API returned no value for that row. We leave it blank.

How is it different from "related keywords" and "phrases containing a keyword"?

Related keywords widen by following relations. Phrases that contain a keyword must contain your word. This one widens by genre grouping.
