Why use this tool

  • Thumbnails in a row. See the trend along with each title and source page URL.
  • 100 results for $0.002. In our test even 100 results cost about $0.002, so you can look beyond the first few.
  • About 7–10 seconds. We measured about 9 seconds for "ebook cover design". Swap keywords and compare several topics.
  • A first step for a new cover. With no monthly fee, compare the trends for several topics the day you start.
  • Save as CSV. Keep the source page URLs and revisit them later.

When it is not the right fit

  • Depending on the keyword, the results can be design-service samples rather than real book covers (in our sample for "ebook cover design", most of the 100 results were cover-design services, templates and marketplace listings such as Etsy and Fiverr). Try other words, such as a genre plus "cover".
  • You want a high-resolution copy or to use the image → the table shows small search-result thumbnails and the source page URL. Other people's images are copyrighted; use them as reference only.
  • You want to compare covers by sales → image-search rank is unrelated to sales. Amazon's bestseller lists fit better.
  • You want to keep tracking where your own image ranks → a rank-tracking tool such as Ahrefs Rank Tracker fits better.

This tool does one thing: show which images lead Google Images for a keyword, as many as you ask for.

FAQ

Can I use a displayed image for my own cover?

No. Images are copyrighted. Treat them as a reference for trends. The tool shows search-result thumbnails only.

Does the rank relate to sales?

No. It is the order in Google Images.

The source page URL has a long string on the end.

The URL is shown exactly as Google returned it. The page still opens normally.

Does a larger count cost more?

In our test, 20 results and 100 results both cost about $0.002.
