Why use this tool

  • Registration and expiry dates in one row. ichiran.com showed registration on 2002-05-31, changed on 2026-05-15 and expiry on 2027-05-31 (measured).
  • The registrar too. ichiran.com was registered through Onamae.com (GMO Internet) (measured).
  • Paste a URL if you like. It is converted to the domain before the lookup.
  • About 2 to 4 seconds. That is what we measured.
  • Save as CSV. Keep a record of a domain's age.

When it is not the right fit

  • The price per call is high → about $0.1212 per call. For many domains, a free tool such as ICANN Lookup fits better.
  • You want the expiry of .co.jp or .ne.jp → the two we tried (rakuten.co.jp, xserver.ne.jp) had a blank expiry (measured).
  • You want the owner's name or contact → the table does not show them.
  • What "changed date" means → it is the "record changed" date DataForSEO returns, not necessarily the date of a renewal.

This tool does one thing: show one domain's registration date, changed date, expiry date and registrar.

FAQ

Why is the expiry blank in some rows?

No expiry came back. Both the .co.jp and .ne.jp domains we tried were blank. We have not confirmed why.

Why is the price high?

It is DataForSEO's pricing: about $0.1212 per call. Think about how many calls you need before you start.

Can I enter a subdomain or www?

It is converted to the plain domain (example.com) before the lookup.

How do I get the domain's age?

Count the years from the registration date to today. This tool does not calculate it.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
