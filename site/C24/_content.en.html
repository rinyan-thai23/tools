Why use this tool

  • Check many keywords together. Line up several post ideas and compare them in one call.
  • Up to 1,000 keywords for $0.09. That is about $0.00009 per keyword. In our U.S. sample "how to start a blog" showed 6,600 searches a month at a $6.06 CPC and "side hustle" 60,500. A 4-keyword and a 538-keyword list cost the same (measured).
  • Over 1,000 is fine. The tool splits the list into batches of 1,000 and sends them one after another. 1,200 keywords took 2 calls, cost $0.18, took about 10 seconds and came back as 1,200 rows (measured).
  • One number, not a range. Instead of a range such as 100–1K, you get a value such as 210 or 320 (keywords with very little search show a blank).
  • CPC and competition come along. A clue to what the keyword is worth to advertisers.
  • Save as CSV. Sort your candidates in a spreadsheet.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want to know how hard it is to rank → the "competition" column is Google Ads competition (0–100), not SEO difficulty.
  • You want to check just one keyword cheaply → one call is $0.09 even for one keyword. It pays off with many keywords.
  • You enter keywords with little search → the volume comes back blank. In our test about 60% of 538 phrases were blank. Blank keywords cost the same.
  • You want more than 5,000 keywords → the tool's limit is 5,000 (5 calls, $0.45).
  • You are fine with free → if rough numbers are enough, the free Keyword Planner does the job.
  • CPC is in US dollars. The tool does not convert it.

This tool does one thing: tabulate monthly volume, CPC, competition and a 12-month trend for the keywords you enter.

FAQ

In what order is the 12-month trend?

From the newest month to the oldest, as "5400 / 8100 / …". In our sample the first value is October 2026.

How many keywords can I enter?

One call carries up to 1,000. Beyond that the tool splits the list into batches of 1,000 and sends them one after another (up to 5,000 in total, 5 calls, $0.45). The screen shows the keyword count, the number of calls and the approximate cost. We tested 4, 538 and 1,200 (2 calls).

Why are some volumes blank?

Google does not show a figure for keywords with very little search. It does not mean nobody searches them.

What is "competition"?

The API's Google Ads competition figure (0–100). A higher number means more advertisers bid on the keyword.

Are the numbers the same as Google Keyword Planner?

The source is Google Ads data, but the way it is shown may differ.
