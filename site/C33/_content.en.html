Why use this tool

  • The breakdown by country in one call. "ramen" returned 139 countries (measured; the United States about 18%, India about 9%). A Japanese-language keyword returned only 52 countries in a separate test (measured in Japan with a Japanese keyword; Japan about 96%).
  • Share included. See at once how lopsided it is.
  • A few seconds. We measured about 0.1 seconds for the US sample.
  • Other languages work too. The number of countries depends on the word.
  • Save as CSV.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want to test many ideas cheaply → at $0.18 per call the price is on the high side.
  • You want country names → countries stay as ISO codes (US, IN and so on). We do not convert them.
  • You want states or cities within a country → this is country level only.
  • The numbers are the API's estimates of search volume.
  • You want only the top countries → every returned country is shown: 139 for "ramen" in our US sample.
  • Each call fetches one keyword; there is no country or language setting.

This tool does one thing: show a keyword's search volume and share by country.

FAQ

Why is it $0.18?

That is the API's price per call. Sending 2 keywords to the API also cost $0.18, and so did a single keyword.

What are the country codes?

US is the United States and IN is India, as two-letter ISO codes.

Why is there no country or language selector?

The numbers cover all countries worldwide.

What is the share a share of?

Each country's part of the total of the returned countries (US 80,109 ÷ total 437,044 = 18.33%, checked for "ramen").
