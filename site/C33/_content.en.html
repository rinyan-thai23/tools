Why use this tool

  • The breakdown by country in one call. "ラーメン" returned 52 countries (measured; Japan about 96%).
  • Share included. See at once how lopsided it is.
  • About 1 second.
  • English words work too. "ramen" returned 139 countries.
  • Save as CSV.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want to test many ideas cheaply → at $0.18 per call the price is on the high side.
  • You want country names → countries stay as ISO codes (JP, US and so on). We do not convert them.
  • You want prefectures within Japan → this is country level. "See popularity by prefecture" fits better.
  • The numbers are the API's estimates of search volume.
  • You want only the top countries → every returned country is shown: 52 for ラーメン and 139 for ramen.
  • Each call fetches one keyword; there is no country or language setting.

This tool does one thing: show a keyword's search volume and share by country.

FAQ

Why is it $0.18?

That is the API's price per call. Sending 2 keywords to the API also cost $0.18 (this tool takes one).

What are the country codes?

JP is Japan and US is the United States, as two-letter ISO codes.

Why is there no country or language selector?

The numbers cover all countries worldwide.

What is the share a share of?

Each country's part of the total of the returned countries (Japan 291,183 ÷ total 302,740 = 96.18%, checked).
