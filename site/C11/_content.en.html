Why use this tool

  • A domain is enough. No need to find the advertiser ID first — type the competitor's site.
  • About $0.002 for 40 results. About 500 lookups per $1, so you can check each client casually.
  • See the run period. First and last shown dates hint at long-running ads versus recent ones.
  • About 3 seconds. Quick enough to check before a meeting.
  • Save as CSV. Paste it into a draft report.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want to read every ad's text → the table gives a preview image and a format; the copy is not returned as text.
  • You want only that company's ads → ads pointing to the domain come back from any advertiser, so others mix in (nike.com returned three advertisers). Use the advertiser column to tell them apart.
  • You want ad results such as clicks or spend → this tool cannot show them.
  • Image and video ads may preview poorly.

This tool does one thing: list the ads currently pointing to a domain.

FAQ

Why is the advertiser different from the domain owner?

Ads pointing to the domain are collected regardless of advertiser, so overseas entities and agencies can appear.

Can I choose the number of results?

It is fixed at 40 in this version.

What does the country selector do?

It sets the region where the ads were shown. Choose Japan for ads shown in Japan.

If the last-shown date is today, is the ad live now?

In our sample, most ads had the fetch date as their last-shown date. That does not guarantee the ad is live.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
