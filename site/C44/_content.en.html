  • About $0.024 per call. About 40 per $1.
  • A few seconds. Under a second in the sample (measured).
  • Save as CSV.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want business rivals → the list is sites that appear in results. YouTube, Reddit and big services mix in, and they are not necessarily your rivals.
  • You want sites that appear for every keyword → sites that appear for only one keyword are listed too.
  • The average position has decimals → it averages positions across keywords. "5.5" can be 5th and 6th.
  • Estimated traffic is a modeled figure from rankings and search volume (per DataForSEO). We have not checked whether it covers only the keywords you entered.

This tool does one thing: show the sites that appear for a set of keywords, with average position and estimated traffic.

FAQ

What is the position an average of?

The site's positions across the keywords you entered. A site that appears for one keyword shows that position as is.

Why 100 by default? And the price?

The tool fetches 100 rows per call by default (you can pick 300 or 1,000 in the count box); the total was 169 in our US sample. The price follows the rows returned: $0.024 for 100 rows, and fewer rows cost less (a test in Japan with Japanese keywords returned 69 rows for $0.020).

How many keywords can I enter?

We confirmed up to 3 here.

Why is "www." missing from domains?

We drop "www." when displaying them.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
