Why use this tool

  • Nearby inns lined up in one call. "京都" returned 20 rows in about 9 to 14 seconds (measured).
  • Price and rating side by side. The rating and the number of reviews appear too.
  • About $0.004 per call. Roughly 250 per $1, so you can refresh every Saturday.
  • The date is fixed to "next Saturday, one night, two adults". You do not have to pick dates.
  • Save as CSV. Use it for a weekly record.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want weekday or holiday prices → the date is fixed to one night from next Saturday.
  • Blank prices → an inn with no price returned is left blank. Of 20 rows, 2 were blank for "京都" and 12 for "東京 ホテル" (measured).
  • You want prices by room type → the table has one price per inn.
  • You want your own inn's rank → the table shows no rank.

This tool does one thing: show the price and rating of inns in an area for one night from next Saturday.

FAQ

What is the price for?

One night for two adults, checking in next Saturday and out on Sunday. The unit is yen.

Why is a price blank?

No price came back for that inn. It could be sold out or have no price for the date, but we cannot tell the reason.

How do I write the area?

A place name like "京都" or "浅草". Adding a word, as in "東京 ホテル", also works.

Can I change the date?

Not in this tool. It is fixed to next Saturday.
