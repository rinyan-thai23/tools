Why use this tool

  • Sentiment shares per page. Positive, negative and neutral shares line up row by row.
  • The total is visible. About 4.97 million Japanese pages matched "ユニクロ" (measured).
  • About $0.0276 per call. That is 100 rows, roughly 36 calls per $1.
  • Save as CSV. Groundwork for a PR report.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want social media posts → this table covers web pages; social posts are not necessarily included (see the official site).
  • You need dates → only 10 of the 100 rows we got had a date (measured). Rows without one are blank.
  • You want only the company's own pages → shopping sites and roundup sites that mention the word appear too.
  • You want automatic daily alerts → this tool shows the result when you press the button. Google Alerts fits better.

FAQ

Which languages are covered?

Japanese pages only. The language filter is fixed by this tool.

What are the sentiment shares?

DataForSEO analyses each page's text and gives positive, negative and neutral shares. A person did not judge them.

Can I see more than 100?

This tool stops at 100. For the total count, use the tool "See a summary of mentions".

Why do unrelated shop pages appear?

Every page containing the keyword is included, including shopping pages that use the same word as a company name.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
