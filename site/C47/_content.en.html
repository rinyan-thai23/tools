Why use this tool

  • Two sites' positions side by side. For "ソルティア", suumo.jp was 4th and homes.co.jp 3rd (measured).
  • Sorted by volume. About 250 thousand shared keywords in total (suumo.jp and homes.co.jp, measured).
  • About $0.024 per call. About 40 per $1.
  • About 1–2 seconds.
  • Save as CSV.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want keywords only the rival has (your gaps) → this tool returns only keywords both appear for.
  • You want to compare three or more sites → two only.
  • You want more than 100 → it stops at 100.
  • Words unique to the field, such as property or company names, can lead the list (measured).

This tool does one thing: show 100 keywords two domains both appear for, with positions and volume.

FAQ

Which is the first site and which the second?

The upper input is the first and the lower the second. The "first site position" and "second site position" columns match them.

Can a position be blank?

Only shared keywords are listed, so both positions are normally filled.

Why only 100? And the price?

The tool fixes the count at 100 per call. The price follows the rows returned: 4 shared keywords cost $0.012 (measured).

What do country and language change?

The market for the numbers. Japan and Japanese give positions in Japanese results.
