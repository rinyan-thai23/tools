Why use this tool

  • Two sites' positions side by side. For "targets", target.com was 1st and walmart.com 11th (measured for target.com and walmart.com).
  • Sorted by volume. About 6.25 million shared keywords in total (target.com and walmart.com, measured); the top row was "targets" at about 45.5 million searches a month.
  • About $0.024 per call. About 40 per $1.
  • A few seconds. The sample call took about 6 seconds (measured).
  • Save as CSV.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want keywords only the rival has (your gaps) → this tool returns only keywords both appear for.
  • You want to compare three or more sites → two only.
  • You want more than 100 → choose 300 or 1,000 in the count field.
  • Brand words can lead the list: for target.com and walmart.com, "targets" came first (measured).

FAQ

Which is the first site and which the second?

The upper input is the first and the lower the second. The "first site position" and "second site position" columns match them.

Can a position be blank?

Only shared keywords are listed, so both positions are normally filled.

Why only 100? And the price?

The default is 100 per call; you can choose 300 or 1,000. The price follows the rows returned: in a small test with Japanese domains, 4 shared keywords cost $0.012 (measured in Japan).

What do country and language change?

The market for the numbers. For example, the United States and English give positions in US results.
