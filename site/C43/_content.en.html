Why use this tool

  • No input needed. A starting point when you are stuck.
  • See the scale of volume. The top words were spellings of "youtube" at 226 million a month, then "amazon" at 151 million (measured, US).
  • About $0.024 per call. About 40 per $1.
  • A few seconds. 4.7 seconds in the sample (measured).
  • Save as CSV.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want what is trending now → this is not a "what is hot" list. Perennially high words such as "youtube" and "amazon" lead.
  • You want words worth writing about → many of the top words, like "youtube" and "amazon", are too broad for an article.
  • Typos get in the way → mistyped words such as "yout7be", "yo0utube" and "yutb" rank near the top (measured). We do not filter them.
  • You want more than 100 → it stops at 100 (about 1.27 billion words in total in the US sample).

This tool does one thing: show the top 100 words by search volume as they are.

FAQ

Is it the same list every time?

It is the top by volume, so it does not change much. "youtube" and its misspellings led in our US sample.

Why do words like "yo0utube" appear?

We show the values the API returned as they are. This tool cannot tell why, and we do not filter them.

What do country and language change?

The market for the numbers. The sample uses the United States and English, so it gives the top words in the US.

Why is there no input field?

The API takes no word; it works from country and language only.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
