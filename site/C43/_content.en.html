Why use this tool

  • No input needed. A starting point when you are stuck.
  • See the scale of volume. The top word, "天気" (weather), had 68 million a month (measured).
  • About $0.024 per call. About 40 per $1.
  • About 2 seconds.
  • Save as CSV.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want what is trending now → this is not a "what is hot" list. Always-high words such as "天気" and "youtube" lead.
  • You want words worth writing about → the most searched words are also the most competitive, and many do not suit an article.
  • Typos get in the way → mistyped words such as "yo0utube", "youtub3" and "ytb" rank near the top (measured). We do not filter them.
  • You want more than 100 → it stops at 100 (3.4 hundred million in total).

This tool does one thing: show the top 100 words by search volume as they are.

FAQ

Is it the same list every time?

It is the top by volume, so it does not change much. "天気" led in our sample.

Why do words like "yo0utube" appear?

Google's advertising data also counts mistyped searches. We show the API's values as they are.

What do country and language change?

The market for the numbers. Japan and Japanese give the top words in Japan.

Why is there no input field?

The API takes no word; it works from country and language only.
