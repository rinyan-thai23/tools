Why use this tool

  • One page becomes a table in seconds. We measured under a second.
  • Heading counts included. The blog article we tried (about 5,054 words) had one H1, 19 H2, 31 H3 and eight H4 (measured with shopify.com/blog/how-to-start-a-blog).
  • About $0.00015 per call. Roughly 6,600 per $1, so checking a client's pages one by one costs very little.
  • Load time is shown. That article loaded in 85 ms (DataForSEO's own measurement).
  • Save as CSV. Use the result as a draft for a quote or a report.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want to check a whole site at once → this tool does one page at a time. Crawler software fits better.
  • You want a speed score → "See a page's speed scores" fits better.
  • You want search rankings → the tables here do not show rankings.
  • Words such as "too long", "too short" and "slow" in the check flags are DataForSEO's own thresholds. See DataForSEO's documentation for the thresholds.

This tool does one thing: show one page's title, description, headings, word count, load time and check flags.

FAQ

How far do you count headings?

H1 through H6. For each level you get the count and the heading text.

What unit is the load time in?

Milliseconds (1,000 ms = 1 second). It is measured on DataForSEO's side, so it can differ from what you see on your own computer.

Does "Yes" in the check flags mean something is wrong?

No. It simply answers the sentence in the item name. If "No H1 tag" says Yes, no H1 was found.

Can it check pages behind a login?

We have not tried it. We tested public pages.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
