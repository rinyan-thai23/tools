Why use this tool

  • See the competitive set at a glance. Ratings, review counts, phones and websites of the top places sit in one table.
  • The same price at any count. In our tests (a US sample and an earlier test in Japan with a Japanese keyword), 10, 20 and 100 results all cost $0.002, so you can check whether you are in the top 100.
  • Fast. Results came back in under 2 seconds in our US sample ("massage san francisco", 10 results), so you can try several keywords.
  • Pay only when you look. No monthly fee, so check on the day you need it — before reprinting flyers or signage, say.
  • Save as CSV. Pull the list monthly and compare how ratings and review counts grow.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want your rank at points around the shop (a grid) → a geo-grid tool such as Local Falcon fits better.
  • You want your rank logged automatically every day → a local-SEO tracking tool fits better. This tool gives one snapshot per request.
  • You need the exact rank someone sees standing next to your shop → this tool uses a country-wide location, so it can differ from the rank on the spot.

FAQ

My shop does not appear.

It may be outside the count you chose. Set the count to 100 and look again. If it still does not appear, the keyword or the shop name may be written differently.

Why does it differ from what I see on the map?

Map ranks change with the searcher's location and the time. This tool uses a country-wide location, so it can differ from a search done right next to your shop.

Does asking for 100 results cost more?

In our tests, 10, 20 and 100 results all cost $0.002 (10 in a US sample; 20 and 100 in an earlier test in Japan).

Some places have no phone or website.

Fields that are not on the place's Google Maps listing are shown blank.
