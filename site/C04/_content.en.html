Why use this tool

  • See rank 4 and beyond. Check where shops outside the top 3 slots stand, with rank numbers.
  • Competitors' ratings and review counts side by side. The gap with your own shop shows in numbers.
  • The description carries area and opening hours. You can tell which area a shop covers and whether it is open, right in the list.
  • No subscription — one look at a turning point. Check how nearby shops line up before you change prices or run an ad.
  • Save as CSV. Pull the list monthly and compare how ranks move.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want the rank of hotels or inns → in an earlier test in Japan with Japanese keywords, "Kyoto hotel" and "Kyoto inn" returned nothing (and were still charged). We did not find out why; one possibility is that lodging is shown by a different Google feature.
  • You want address as its own column → this list has no address field; the area is inside the description.
  • You just want the map results quickly → See the top places on Google Maps is faster (seconds) and includes the address.
  • You want your rank at points around the shop (a grid) → a geo-grid tool such as Local Falcon fits better.

This tool does one thing: show which shops sit in the "more places" list on the map, and in what order, right now.

FAQ

I got "no results". Was I charged?

Yes. In an earlier test in Japan, a no-result request was still charged $0.004 (a normal 20-result request cost $0.002; we do not know why the amounts differ — check the cost shown under the results). It happens with keywords where this list does not appear, such as hotels and inns. Try a service keyword like "massage san francisco".

Why does it take about 2 minutes?

Google now routes each result link through Google first (for click tracking). The tool follows those links to find the real URLs, which takes time. In our US sample ("massage san francisco") 20 results took about 2.5 minutes (153 seconds); in an earlier test in Japan, 20 results took about 2 minutes and 100 about 2.5 minutes.

How is this different from the Google Maps tool?

This one reads the "more places" list behind the map slots in the search results, not the Maps search itself. The order and the set of shops can differ.

Why is there no address?

This list's data has no address field. Area names and opening hours are inside the description.
