Why use this tool

  • Source, date, title and URL in one table. Use the list as-is in a meeting or report.
  • Only as many as you need. Choose 10, 20, 50 or 100 results. In our tests, 10 results cost about $0.002 and 20 cost about $0.004; for larger counts, check the cost shown under the results.
  • No monthly fee. The day after a press release, check which outlets picked it up.
  • Pick country and language. See how you appear in news abroad, too.
  • Save as CSV. Pull it monthly and compare how coverage grows.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want automatic alerts for new articles → Google Alerts fits better (free).
  • You want social mentions or sentiment analysis → a monitoring service such as Brand24 fits better. This tool only lists Google News articles.
  • You want every past article → Google News returns mostly recent articles.

This tool does one thing: show which articles Google News lists right now, as many as you ask for, in a table.

FAQ

Is the date the publication date?

It is the article time Google returns, shown as a date. Google itself shows relative text such as "2 days ago"; this tool shows the returned timestamp as a date.

Why does it take about a minute?

Google now routes each result link through Google first (for click tracking). The tool follows those links to find the real article URLs, which took about 50 seconds to 1 minute in our tests.

How much does a larger count cost?

In our tests, 10 results cost $0.002 and 20 results cost $0.004, We have not measured larger counts; check the cost shown under the results.

Searching my company name shows unrelated articles.

Articles matching the keyword are shown as returned. Adding an industry word to the company name narrows them.
