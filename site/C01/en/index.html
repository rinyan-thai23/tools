When it is not the right fit

  • You want to track your own site's rank every day → a rank tracker fits better.
  • You need keyword difficulty and backlinks in one view → an all-in-one SEO suite such as Ahrefs or Semrush fits better.

This tool does one thing: show the top results — rank, title, URL and domain — as many as you need, when you need them, for the country and language you choose.

FAQ

How much does one search cost?

About $0.004 for 10 results (about 250 per $1) and $0.0055 for 20; cost rises a little as the count grows. The actual cost is shown under the results.

Can I try it for free?

The sample view is free. Real searches need a DataForSEO account; DataForSEO states that new accounts receive a small free credit (check their site for terms).

Why does it take a while?

Google now routes each result link through Google first (for click tracking). The tool follows those links to find the real URLs, which takes about 1–1.5 minutes — longer for more results. An elapsed-time indicator is shown while it runs.

Why do results differ from my own search?

Results vary by location, device, search history and time. This tool fetches results for the country and language you choose, and ranks can shift a little between requests.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
