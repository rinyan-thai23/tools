Why use this tool

  • The answer and its sources, side by side. Keep the cited titles and URLs as they are.
  • Real source URLs. You get the original page URL, not a Google redirect link.
  • About 7 seconds. We measured about 4–8 seconds.
  • Any question you like. Phrase it for your client's industry.
  • Save as CSV. Paste it into a proposal draft.

When it is not the right fit

  • You need a fixed price → with web search the cost varies with answer length and searches. In 3 tests it was $0.016, $0.030 and $0.058.
  • You want exactly what the Gemini app shows → this asks gemini-3.5-flash-lite through the API, so the answer can differ.
  • You call at a busy moment → the service behind DataForSEO sometimes returned a busy error (50301). The tool retries up to 3 times, then says it is busy (no charge in that case).
  • You want ongoing tracking → one call is one answer at one moment.

This tool does one thing: show how web-search Gemini answers a question and what it cites.

FAQ

Why does the cost vary?

With web search, the cost depends on the amount of searching and the answer length. We measured about $0.016 to $0.058.

Why is a source title just a site name?

Gemini often names a citation by its site. We show it as returned.

Am I charged when an error comes back?

For the busy error (50301) the cost was $0.

Why is there no country or language selector?

This call has no country or language setting.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
