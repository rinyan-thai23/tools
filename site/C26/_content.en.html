Why use this tool

  • One keyword grows into many ideas. "ラーメン 渋谷" returned 80 rows and "ラーメン 新宿" returned 191 (measured).
  • Place-name phrases appear. Find words around your shop.
  • Volume, CPC and competition included. A guide to words worth bidding on.
  • A few seconds. We measured about 3–5 seconds.
  • Save as CSV.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want a cost estimate for ads → CPC is a per-click guide in US dollars. Real cost depends on how you run the ads.
  • You want to choose the count → every returned row is shown.
  • What competition means → the competition column is Google Ads competition (0–100), not SEO difficulty.
  • Loosely related words (such as other shop names) mix in.
  • You want to limit it to the area around your shop → it works at country level; you cannot narrow to a city or ward. Put the place name into the keyword instead.
  • The numbers are advertising data, separate from real ad results.

This tool does one thing: list the keywords related to one keyword.

FAQ

Why do other shop names appear?

We show the words Google associates, unfiltered.

What unit is CPC in?

US dollars, a per-click guide.

What do country and language change?

The market the numbers refer to. Japan and Japanese give Japanese volumes.

Can I enter several keywords?

This tool takes one. For several at once, "see search volume" fits better.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
