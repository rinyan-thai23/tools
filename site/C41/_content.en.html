Why use this tool

  • Five years of monthly numbers. "how to start a blog" returned 62 months (measured in the US).
  • Search counts, not popularity. Unlike Google Trends, these are volume numbers.
  • About $0.012 per call. About 80 per $1.
  • A few seconds. We measured under 1 second for the call.
  • Save as CSV. Overlay years in a spreadsheet chart.

When it is not the right fit

  • You need no missing months → 62 months came back, so one month in the span was missing (measured).
  • You want the 12-month average → this table shows each month's own volume (the API also holds a 12-month average per date; we do not show it).
  • You want to compare several keywords → one keyword per call.
  • You want a forecast → only past numbers.

This tool does one thing: fetch the past monthly search volume of one keyword.

FAQ

What does "volume" count?

An estimate of how many times the keyword was searched in that month. We show the monthly value.

Why separate the monthly value from the 12-month average?

For each month the API returns both that month's value and the 12-month average at that date. For finding peak seasons, the monthly value fits better.

How far back does it go?

August 2021 in our sample.

What do country and language change?

The market for the numbers. For example, the US and English give US numbers, as in our sample.
