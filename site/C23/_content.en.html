Why use this tool

  • Only the AI number. See how much is asked of AI, separate from Google search.
  • A 12-month trend. A hint whether a topic is seasonal or a passing spike.
  • About $0.01, 1–2 seconds. About 99 per $1, so you can check many topic ideas.
  • Pick country and language. See Japanese numbers.
  • Save as CSV.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want the Google search volume → this tool is the AI number. Google Keyword Planner and similar tools fit better.
  • You need exact numbers → the tool shows the API's estimate as returned.
  • You want several keywords at once → one keyword per call.
  • A word nobody asks about shows 12 months of zeros and still costs one call (confirmed in our test).

This tool does one thing: show a keyword's monthly AI search volume over 12 months.

FAQ

What does "AI search volume" count?

The API's estimate of how often the keyword comes up in conversations with AI. This tool cannot tell how it is calculated.

Why do some keywords show zeros?

A word with no sign of being asked of AI shows zeros. A call is still charged.

What is the month column?

Year and month such as "2026-10". The top row is the latest month.

How is it different from Google search volume?

Google volume counts searches in Google. This counts conversations with AI.
