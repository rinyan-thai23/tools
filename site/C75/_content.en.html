Why use this tool

  • Narrowed to Japanese sites. Japanese sites using Shopify numbered 6,788 (measured).
  • Ten prospects in one call. It returned in about 5 to 17 seconds (measured).
  • About $0.024 per call. That is for 10 rows, roughly 41 calls per $1.
  • Pick the technology name from suggestions. Typing "shopify" in lowercase is corrected to "Shopify".
  • Save as CSV. A draft for your sales list.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want more than 10 → this tool stops at 10 to keep the price down.
  • You want contact details → the table has only domain, country and title.
  • You want to choose which 10 → the 10 are in the order DataForSEO returns.
  • The name is not on the list → a name that is not in the list is rejected before any charge.

This tool does one thing: show ten Japanese sites that use a technology, and how many match in total.

FAQ

Why only Japanese sites?

The tool is meant for finding prospects in Japan, so the country is fixed to Japan. This filter is a rule of this tool.

Why only 10 rows?

To keep the price down. 10 rows cost about $0.024.

Where do I look up technology names?

Start typing in the box and suggestions appear, taken from DataForSEO's free list of technology names.

Does it matter whether I use capitals?

DataForSEO is case-sensitive: lowercase returned 0 rows and was still charged (measured). This tool fixes the spelling from the list before sending.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
