When it is not the right fit

  • You expect the same number as another tool → each tool calculates difficulty its own way (Ahrefs' own article notes the same keyword can score very differently). Do not set numbers from different tools side by side.
  • A keyword has no difficulty → DataForSEO says keyword difficulty needs SERP data, so it is only available for keywords in its SERP database.The 3 words we entered all returned a value. Re-entering 8 words that were blank in C34 also came back blank (measured).
  • You also want volume → this table shows difficulty only. "See search volume" fits for volume.
  • Difficulty is a guide. Whether you can reach the top also depends on your content, backlinks and more.

This tool does one thing: show the difficulty (0–100) of several keywords at once.

FAQ

What is the 0–100 number?

DataForSEO describes it as its own score for how hard it is to reach the top 10 results, with 0 easy and 100 extremely hard.

Is it the same as Ahrefs' difficulty?

Not necessarily. The calculations differ, so the same keyword can score differently.

How many keywords can I enter?

DataForSEO's spec says up to 1000. We confirmed up to 3 here.

Why do some keywords show nothing?

DataForSEO says keyword difficulty needs SERP data, so it is only available for keywords in its SERP database.The 3 words we entered all returned a value. Re-entering 8 words that were blank in C34 also came back blank (measured).
