Why use this tool

  • See it page by page. Find which articles earn from search.
  • Sorted by estimated traffic. For suumo.jp the top page was a rental listing page for Fukui, with about 263 thousand (measured).
  • Keyword counts included. See how many keywords a page appears for.
  • About $0.024 per call. About 40 per $1.
  • Save as CSV.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want articles only → on big sites, non-article pages such as prefecture listing pages lead (measured on suumo.jp).
  • You want real visits → Estimated traffic (ETV) is a modeled estimate from rankings and search volume (per DataForSEO), not measured visits, and the calculation may be updated (see the official site).
  • You want more than 100 → it stops at 100 (suumo.jp had about 173 thousand pages in total).
  • Long URLs with parameters can appear.

This tool does one thing: show the 100 pages of a domain with the most estimated search traffic.

FAQ

In what order are the rows?

By estimated traffic, descending. The tool sorts them that way.

What is the keyword count?

The number of keywords for which the page appears in results.

Why do listing pages lead?

This tool cannot tell why. For suumo.jp, a rental listing page for Fukui showed about 263 thousand estimated traffic from 2 keywords (measured). On other sites articles may lead.

What do country and language change?

The market for the numbers. Japan and Japanese give numbers for Japanese results.
