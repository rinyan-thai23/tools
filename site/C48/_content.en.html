Why use this tool

  • See it page by page. See which articles have the most estimated search traffic (an estimate).
  • Sorted by estimated traffic. For target.com the top page was the home page, with about 24.9 million (measured).
  • Keyword counts included. See how many keywords a page appears for.
  • About $0.024 per call. About 40 per $1.
  • Save as CSV.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want articles only → on big sites, non-article pages lead: for target.com the top three were the home page, a store locator page and a careers page (measured).
  • You want real visits → Estimated traffic (ETV) is a modeled estimate from rankings and search volume (per DataForSEO), not measured visits, and the calculation may be updated (see the official site).
  • You want more than 100 → choose 300 or 1,000 in the count field (target.com had about 1.35 million pages in total).
  • Long URLs with parameters can appear.

This tool does one thing: show the 100 pages of a domain with the most estimated search traffic.

FAQ

In what order are the rows?

By estimated traffic, descending. The tool sorts them that way.

What is the keyword count?

The number of keywords for which the page appears in results.

Why do non-article pages lead?

This tool cannot tell why. For target.com, the home page showed about 24.9 million estimated traffic from 2,271 keywords (measured). On other sites articles may lead.

What do country and language change?

The market for the numbers. For example, the United States and English give numbers for US results.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
