Why use this tool

  • Close to the screen. The answer, the sources and the recommended products come together.
  • About $0.004. About 250 per $1, so you can check many keywords.
  • Product name and tag. See how each was introduced, such as "best overall" or "best value" (sample).
  • See Japanese results. Choose country and language.
  • Save as CSV. Keep the sources table.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want a quick result → it takes about 30 seconds (24–31 in our tests) because it searches before it answers.
  • You want product prices or URLs → the product table shows name, tag and image. Price and URL were mostly empty in our sample.
  • You want a verdict on whether your product appeared → the tool only lays out what came back.
  • You want ongoing tracking → one call is one answer at one moment.

This tool does one thing: show how ChatGPT search answers a keyword, what it cites and which products it shows.

FAQ

Why does it take 30 seconds?

It searches first and then writes the answer. We measured about 24–31 seconds.

Which model is it?

The API reported the model as gpt-6. The result is not guaranteed to match the ChatGPT app.

How is it different from "see ChatGPT's answer"?

That tool asks a ChatGPT model directly through the API. This one fetches a result shaped like the ChatGPT search screen, with sources and products.

May I use the product images?

The images come from ChatGPT. Other people's images are copyrighted; use them as reference only.

What does the country and language choice change?

It runs the ChatGPT search for that country and language.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
