Why use this tool

  • Four numbers in one row. Volume, CPC, difficulty and intent.
  • About $0.012 per call. About 80 per $1.
  • A few seconds. We measured under 1 second for the call.
  • Intent in plain words.
  • Save as CSV.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want to compare several keywords → this tool takes one per call. "See search volume" or "see keyword difficulty" fit better for several.
  • CPC is in US dollars. The tool does not convert it.
  • Some keywords have no difficulty → DataForSEO says keyword difficulty needs SERP data, so it is only available for keywords in its SERP database. The one word we entered ("how to start a blog") returned a value, 38 (measured in the US).
  • No 12-month trend is shown. "See monthly volume history" fits for trends.
  • What the volume means → the monthly volume is close to the average of the last 12 months (measured for "how to start a blog" in the US: 6,600 against a 12-month average of about 6,940). It is not the latest month's value.

This tool does one thing: show one keyword's volume, CPC, difficulty and intent in a row.

FAQ

Is intent just one value?

Only the main one is shown, the strongest even if the keyword has mixed intent.

What unit is CPC in?

US dollars, a per-click guide.

Why is difficulty sometimes blank?

DataForSEO says keyword difficulty needs SERP data, so it is only available for keywords in its SERP database. The one word we entered ("how to start a blog") returned a value, 38 (measured in the US).

What do country and language change?

The market for the numbers. For example, the US and English give US numbers, as in our sample.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
