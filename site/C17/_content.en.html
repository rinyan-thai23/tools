When it is not the right fit

  • The video has no subtitles → we did not test videos without subtitles.
  • You want a summary or translation → the tool returns the subtitles as they are.
  • You need another language → only Japanese and English are selectable in this version.
  • Automatic subtitles can contain mishearings.

This tool does one thing: make a video's subtitles readable in full with times.

FAQ

Were the subtitles made by the uploader?

We show what the API returned and do not tell uploaded from automatic ones.

Do long videos cost more?

A 3.5-minute video and a Japanese video with 212 subtitle lines each cost $0.006. Videos over an hour are untested.

What is the time column?

The start of that subtitle line, in minutes and seconds such as "3:25".

If I choose Japanese, will an English video come out in Japanese?

What happens when the chosen language has no subtitles on that video is unconfirmed. Try the other language per video.
