Why use this tool

  • Compare at the article (URL) level. See the overlap between articles, not whole sites.
  • Positions side by side. For two Tokyo rental pages (suumo.jp and homes.co.jp), "東京賃貸" was 1st and 2nd (measured).
  • 128 shared keywords. These two pages shared 128 (measured).
  • About $0.024 per call. About 40 per $1.
  • Save as CSV.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want keywords only one page has (your gaps) → only keywords both appear for are returned.
  • When nothing is shared → the table is empty but the call still costs $0.012 (confirmed with a suumo.jp article and another homes.co.jp article).
  • Near-duplicate keywords repeat → rows like "東京の賃貸" can differ only by spacing or spelling and appear several times (measured).
  • You want more than 100 → it stops at 100.

This tool does one thing: show 100 keywords two URLs both appear for, with positions and volume.

FAQ

Do I enter domains?

No, article (page) URLs. Enter two URLs starting with "https://".

Why can the result be empty?

The two pages do not both appear for any same keyword. The call is still charged.

Why does the same keyword repeat?

The API treats differences in spacing or spelling as different keywords. We show them as they come.

In what order are the rows?

By search volume, descending. The tool sorts them that way.
