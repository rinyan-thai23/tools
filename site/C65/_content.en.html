FAQ

Why is the count 4,000,000?

For heavily mentioned words like "ユニクロ" the count stops at 4,000,000 (measured). Treat it as a ceiling, not the real number.

Can I filter by language?

Not in this tool. It shows counts per language in a table instead.

How is the sentiment decided?

DataForSEO analyses each page's text. A person did not judge it.

Are the top domains the company's official sites?

No. They are the sites with the most pages containing the keyword, which need not be official sites.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
