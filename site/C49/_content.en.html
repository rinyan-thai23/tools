Why use this tool

  • A prospect's size in numbers. suumo.jp showed about 19.9 million in estimated traffic and about 340 thousand keywords (measured).
  • Counts per position band too. See how many keywords rank 1st and how many reach the top 10.
  • About $0.012 per call. About 80 per $1, so you can check many prospects.
  • About 1 second. That is what we measured on screen.
  • Save as CSV.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want real visit counts → estimated traffic is a modeled figure from rankings and search volume (per DataForSEO), not real visitors, and the calculation may be updated (see the official site).
  • You want ads (paid) numbers → the tables show organic search only.
  • You want traffic from outside search (social, direct) → this tool cannot show it.
  • You want a trend → one call is one snapshot. "See a site's estimated traffic trend" fits better.

This tool does one thing: show a domain's estimated search traffic, keyword count and count per position band.

FAQ

What is a "position band"?

A grouping of keywords by rank: 1st, 2nd–3rd, 4th–10th, 11th–20th … 91st–100th, twelve bands in all.

Does estimated traffic include ads?

No, organic search only. The API also returns ad figures, which this tool does not show.

Why two tables?

Traffic and keyword count are one row; the position bands are twelve rows.

What do country and language change?

The market for the numbers. Japan and Japanese give numbers for Japanese results.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
