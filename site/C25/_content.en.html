Why use this tool

  • A domain is enough. Type it as "example.com".
  • 998 rows in one call. rakuten.co.jp returned 998 (one test), all for $0.09.
  • Volume, CPC and competition included. Useful for prioritizing ideas.
  • A few seconds. We measured about 5–8 seconds.
  • Save as CSV. Filter in a spreadsheet.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want the rankings the site actually holds → these are Google Ads keyword ideas, not the site's rankings.
  • You find too many rows → all rows are shown. The page gets long; export to CSV and filter in a spreadsheet.
  • What competition means → the competition column is Google Ads competition (0–100), not SEO difficulty.
  • Some rows have no CPC → 140 of 998 rows for rakuten.co.jp (measured; the sample holds only the first 30). They stay blank.
  • Brand and product names (brand searches) mix in as they come.
  • Some domains return 0 rows → suumo.jp returned none, and the call still cost $0.09 (measured).

This tool does one thing: list keyword ideas related to a domain, with their volume.

FAQ

Are these the keywords the site ranks for?

No. They are Google Ads keyword ideas, not the site's rankings.

Why so many rows?

We show every row the API returns. You cannot choose the count.

Why do Japanese and English mix?

Words related to the site appear as they are. Some rows are English, such as "rakuten jp".

What does a blank CPC mean?

The API returned no value for that row. We leave it blank.

Am I charged when there are 0 rows?

Yes. suumo.jp returned no rows but cost $0.09 (measured).

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
