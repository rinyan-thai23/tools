Why use this tool

  • Look back in time. See about a year of line-ups even if you never recorded them.
  • About $0.0005–0.0007 per call. About 1,400 or more per $1, so you can try many keywords.
  • See whether #1 changes. For "ブログ 始め方", #1 was xserver.ne.jp at all six snapshots, while the rest rotated (measured).
  • About 1 second. That is what we measured on screen.
  • Save as CSV.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want a monthly trend → results come at the captured moments, not monthly. "ブログ 始め方" had six: Nov 2025, Jan, Mar, Jun, Sep and Nov 2026 (measured).
  • You want rank 11 and below → only the top 10 per snapshot are shown; each stored SERP holds about 100 results, but the table omits them.
  • You want other result blocks (AI overviews, related questions, video) → only organic results are shown.
  • You want daily changes → snapshots are months apart.
  • The same domain can appear several times within one snapshot's top 10 (different pages). We show them as they come.

This tool does one thing: show a keyword's top-10 results over the past year, snapshot by snapshot.

FAQ

Why is it not monthly?

The API's history only holds the moments it captured. The number and spacing of snapshots may differ by keyword.

Why only the top 10?

To serve as a "who is on top" tool, we limit it to the top 10. That limit is this tool's own rule.

How much does it cost?

About $0.0007 for six snapshots in our test of this tool; it may vary a little with the number of snapshots.

What do country and language change?

The market for the numbers. Japan and Japanese give the history of Japanese results.
