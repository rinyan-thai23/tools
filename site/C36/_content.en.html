When it is not the right fit

  • Spelling-only variants get in the way → rows like "ブログ 始め 方", "ブログの始め方" and "始め方 ブログ" appear almost the same. We do not filter them.
  • You want a table with every difficulty filled in → DataForSEO says keyword difficulty needs SERP data, so it is only available for keywords in its SERP database.In our test ("ブログ 始め方"), 11 of 100 rows were blank.
  • You want more than 100 → it stops at 100.
  • Similar-meaning words that do not contain your word do not appear (the "keywords in the same genre" tool fits that).

FAQ

Why do near-identical phrases repeat?

The API treats a different space or word order as a different keyword. We show them as they come.

Why are many difficulty values blank?

DataForSEO says keyword difficulty needs SERP data, so it is only available for keywords in its SERP database.In our test ("ブログ 始め方"), 11 of 100 rows were blank.

Can I see all 740?

No. The tool fixes the count at 100.

What do country and language change?

The market for the numbers. Japan and Japanese give Japanese volumes.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
