Why use this tool

  • Long combinations in a row. See long-tail candidates together.
  • The total is visible. Phrases containing "how to start a blog" totaled 1,675 (measured).
  • About $0.024 for 100. About 40 per $1.
  • A few seconds. We measured under 1 second for the US sample.
  • Save as CSV.

When it is not the right fit

  • Spelling-only variants get in the way → rows like "how to start a blog", "how to start a blog blog" and "start a blog how to" appear almost the same. We do not filter them.
  • You want a table with every difficulty filled in → DataForSEO says keyword difficulty needs SERP data, so it is only available for keywords in its SERP database. In our US test ("how to start a blog"), none of the 30 rows kept in the sample were blank, but blanks do occur: in a test in Japan with a Japanese keyword, 11 of 100 rows were blank.
  • You want more than 100 → it stops at 100.
  • Similar-meaning words that do not contain your word do not appear (the "keywords in the same genre" tool fits that).

This tool does one thing: show up to 100 keywords that contain your keyword, with volume and difficulty.

FAQ

Why do near-identical phrases repeat?

The API treats a different space or word order as a different keyword. We show them as they come.

Why is difficulty sometimes blank?

DataForSEO says keyword difficulty needs SERP data, so it is only available for keywords in its SERP database. In our US test ("how to start a blog"), none of the 30 rows kept in the sample were blank, but blanks do occur: in a test in Japan with a Japanese keyword, 11 of 100 rows were blank.

Can I see all 1,675?

No. The tool fixes the count at 100.

What do country and language change?

The market for the numbers. The United States and English give US English volumes.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
