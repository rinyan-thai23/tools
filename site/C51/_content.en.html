Why use this tool

  • A monthly trend in a table. suumo.jp showed about 26.8 million in December 2025 and about 23.4 million in October 2026 (measured).
  • About $0.134 for 12 months. Higher per call than other tools, but you get a full year at once.
  • Works on other people's sites. See a rival's momentum too.
  • About 1 second. That is what we measured on screen.
  • Save as CSV. Make your own chart.

When it is not the right fit

  • You want to test cheaply and often → at about $0.134 per call this is on the high side for this series.
  • You want real visit counts → estimated traffic is a modeled figure from rankings and search volume (per DataForSEO), not real visitors.
  • You read the whole series as a drop → for suumo.jp, estimated traffic fell from about 26.8 million to about 19.7 million over the 12 months, and keywords from about 690 thousand to about 370 thousand (measured). Check against other measures before drawing conclusions.
  • You trust the latest month → the latest month can come out lower than the one before. We have not checked whether it is a partial month (suumo.jp: about 19.7 million in November 2026 against about 23.4 million in October).
  • You want more than 12 months → the tool stops at 12.

This tool does one thing: show a domain's monthly estimated traffic and keyword count for the past 12 months.

FAQ

Why is one call so expensive?

The API's price per call is high. It does not scale with the months: with no date set, six months cost $0.127; twelve months cost $0.134 (measured).

Which 12 months?

The twelve months ending with the month you run it. The start date is set automatically from today.

Does a low latest month mean the site dropped?

We cannot say. It may be a partial month, but we have not checked.

What do country and language change?

The market for the numbers. Japan and Japanese give numbers for Japanese results.

Can I sign up with a free email address?

No. DataForSEO does not accept free email providers. See What is DataForSEO?
